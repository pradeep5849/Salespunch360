# A121-F04 — Preserve confirmed attendance through device failures

Device tracking and upload scheduling failures are now recoverable warnings after a server-confirmed attendance response. They cannot turn a committed start/stop into a request-failure message, prevent the other device effect from running, or prevent bootstrap from installing current authorized state. Cancellation still propagates. Warnings include safe recovery guidance and survive the post-save bootstrap refresh. All three attendance surfaces delegate tracking to the ViewModel instead of starting/stopping it again in success callbacks.

Eight JVM cases cover success, tracking SecurityException with upload recovery, scheduler failure with tracking preserved, cancellation, recovered server-time start, stop without invented timestamp, unchanged/unconfirmed state, and a different active session. Whitespace passed; native compilation/JVM/lint CI and real permission/network/device acceptance are pending. Previous combined JS regression: 4266 passed / 454 gated skipped. Lost responses now trigger a fresh bootstrap without replaying the mutation. Recovered messages describe current authoritative state rather than claiming this device committed a transition. Missing refresh enters recoverable-error state; changed Sales/field authority prevents a false stopped-state claim. Final-head native checks and real device/network acceptance remain pending. No API/policy or production data/settings change, deployment or merge.
